# Find Intersection (LL)

Source: HackerRank Style Unit 6 Assessment, Version A

## Question

Given the heads of two singly linked lists, return the node at which the two lists intersect. If the two linked
lists do not intersect, return None. You may not modify either of the linked lists.

EXAMPLE
list_a is     a1->a2->c1->c2->c3
list_b is b1->b2->b3->c1->c2->c3
intersection point is c1
